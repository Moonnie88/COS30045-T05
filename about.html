About Us

PowerWise is a student website for COS30045 Data Visualisation (Lab T05). It helps Australian households buying a TV understand what it will cost to run.

Our Mission

To show, with clear charts, how screen technology, star rating and power prices affect a TV's running cost.

Our Data

The charts use the CSV files provided with COS30045 Exercise 5: TV energy use by brand, screen technology, size and star rating, and yearly electricity spot prices from 1998 to 2024.

How It's Built

Each chart is drawn with D3.js from its own JavaScript file and redraws itself when the browser window changes size.
